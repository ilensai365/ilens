FAQ

Good to know

What types of shoots do you cover?

iGaming and trade-show stands, conferences, award nights, product launches, company and private events, team headshots, and real estate — apartments, villas and commercial buildings.

Where are you available?

Anywhere in Malta and Gozo with no travel fee, and across Europe for expos and events — travel and accommodation are quoted upfront.

How fast will I get the photos?

For events, a first set of highlights can be delivered during or right after the event for your socials; the full edited gallery follows within a few days. Timings are confirmed in your quote.

How do you deliver the files?

Through an online gallery with high-resolution downloads, plus web-sized versions ready for LinkedIn, Instagram and your website.

Are edits included?

Yes — every delivered photo is selected, colour-corrected and edited. Logo-branded exports are available on request.

Can I use the photos commercially?

Yes, business clients get a licence to use the photos for marketing, PR and social media. The details are in your quote.

Can you cover multi-day expos?

Yes. ICE London and SiGMA-style shows are usually booked as full days across the whole event, with daily selects.

Do you invoice companies?

Yes, I invoice your company for every booking.
