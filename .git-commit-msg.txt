Rework visual identity: bespoke iLens graphics over old portfolio

Reduced old portfolio photography from 6 pieces down to 3
(Brand Identity, Sports, Campaigns), kept only in a small
"Selected Experience" strip sized and weighted like the What-I-Do
cards -- clearly secondary to iLens' own visual identity, not a
gallery. Dropped Editorial, Travel and Commercial pieces entirely.

Hero and About no longer use any photography. Both now use a
bespoke inline-SVG aperture/lens motif (concentric rings + radial
spokes) in the site's own ivory/black/taupe palette -- original to
iLens, nothing sourced from the founder's old portfolio, Instagram
or Facebook. Hero renders it light-on-ivory blended into the
existing background gradient; About renders a inverted version
light-on-black inside the existing framed panel.

Digital Products, Method and Services are untouched -- already
bespoke CSS/SVG with no photography.

Net effect: only 3 real images remain in the whole site, all
confined to Selected Experience and explicitly captioned as past
work, not current iLens client work.
