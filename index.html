About

A creative eye,
a strategist's mind.

I'm the founder of iLens — a creative director and strategist with a Master's degree in Graphic Design and a background as a professional photographer and visual creator.

Over the years I've worked across marketing, branding and customer experience, including inside high-stakes, internationally regulated environments — with experience at bet365 and Betclic, working with global customers and complex compliance requirements in the iGaming industry. That work taught me precision, discretion and how to build brand experiences that hold up under scrutiny.

Today, iLens is where all of that comes together: design craft, strategic thinking, project management discipline and a deep, hands-on interest in AI-assisted creative workflows — applied to help brands and individuals move with clarity and intent.
